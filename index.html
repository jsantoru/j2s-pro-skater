Revert: Tap either trigger to switch stance. Timed ramp landings add +1x; ordinary ground taps add no score
Scoring — (sum of trick points) × multiplier. Every trick in the chain adds 1x, and every full 360 of spin adds another. Rotation pays into the trick you threw it with: 180 +100, 360 +250, 540 +450, 720 +700. Holding pays too — 100/s on a rail, 50/s in a manual, 100/s on a held grab.
Repeat a trick in one combo and it decays to 75% → 50% → 25% → 10%. Landing switch is worth 1.2× and counts as a different trick, so it wipes that decay. Variety pays; spamming does not.
