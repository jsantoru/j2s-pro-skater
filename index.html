    CONTROLS

    TWO THUMBS. YOUR LINE.

    Use the left stick to steer and the buttons on the right for tricks. Keep both thumbs on the screen.

    Stick ← →Steer, spin in the air, or balance a grind.
    Stick ↑ / ↓Push for speed / brake. In a manual, balance the pitch.
    OLLIEHold to crouch and build speed. Release to jump; hold longer to jump higher.
    FLIP / GRABTap FLIP or hold GRAB with a stick direction to choose your trick.
    GRINDTap while airborne near a rail. Balance with the stick.
    Stick ↓↑ flickManual. Flick ↑↓ for a Nose Manual.
    REVERTSwitch stance. Revert on a ramp landing, then manual, to keep your combo.

    Landscape gives your thumbs more room. Portrait works too.

    Manuals (100): Manual · Nose Manual
    Revert: Tap either trigger to switch stance. Timed ramp landings add +1x; ordinary ground taps add no score
    Scoring — (sum of trick points) × multiplier. Every trick in the chain adds 1x, and every full 360 of spin adds another. Rotation pays into the trick you threw it with: 180 +100, 360 +250, 540 +450, 720 +700. Holding pays too — 100/s on a rail, 50/s in a manual, 100/s on a held grab.
    Repeat a trick in one combo and it decays to 75% → 50% → 25% → 10%. Landing switch is worth 1.2× and counts as a different trick, so it wipes that decay. Variety pays; spamming does not.
